# What makes a post popular

A practical guide (2026-11-07), built from [`trending-research.md`](trending-research.md), which has the
full research, data sources and links. Part 1 covers what works everywhere, part 2 what works per
group of apps. Next to each rule is where it works especially well.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[official]** | The platform said it |
| **[consistent]** | Many independent sources agree |
| **[single]** | One source; treat as a hypothesis and measure it |

---

## 1. What makes a post popular in general

Each rule applies on every app. Under it: where it works especially well.

### 1.1 Hook in the first 1-3 seconds or the first line
Every app first shows a post to a small test group; if they scroll past, it stops there. [consistent]
- **TikTok, Reels, Shorts:** the swipe decision happens in the first 1-3 seconds.
- **YouTube Shorts:** "viewed vs. swiped away" in YouTube Studio measures exactly this. [official metric]
- **LinkedIn:** the first two lines, before "see more".

### 1.2 Make people watch or read to the end
Watch time and completion are the strongest ranking signals. [official for Instagram; consistent elsewhere]
- **TikTok:** completion and rewatches matter most. [consistent]
- **Instagram:** watch time is one of its three top signals. [official]
- **LinkedIn:** dwell time, how long people stop on the post. [official]

### 1.3 Make it worth sending to a friend
Shares and sends push a post to people who don't follow you. [official for Instagram]
- **Instagram:** "sends per reach" is a top signal for reaching non-followers. [official]
- **TikTok:** shares outweigh likes. [consistent]
- **X, Threads:** reposts are the strongest signal. [official for X]

### 1.4 Start a real conversation
Real replies count; emoji-only or "nice!" replies count for almost nothing. [official for X]
- **X:** replies the author answers rank high; low-effort replies score near zero. [official, open-source code]
- **Threads:** replies carry posts to non-followers. [consistent]
- **LinkedIn:** long comments in the first hour. [consistent]

### 1.5 Be original
Reposted content loses recommendations. [official for Instagram]
- **Instagram:** since April 2026, accounts that mostly repost aren't recommended to non-followers. [official]
- **TikTok:** no other app's watermark. [single]
- **YouTube Shorts:** re-uploads get less reach. [single]

### 1.6 Catch the trend early and add your own angle
A trend is worth most in its first 24-48 hours; after that it's saturated. [consistent]
- **TikTok:** use a trending sound in its first ~24 hours. [single]
- **X:** trends move by the hour.

### 1.7 Post when your audience is online, and stay for the first hour
Early engagement decides the next wave of distribution. [consistent]
- **LinkedIn:** the first 60-90 minutes. [consistent]
- **All apps:** the account's own "followers online" data beats any industry average.

### 1.8 Clear keywords, few hashtags
Apps read the caption, on-screen text and speech for search and recommendations. [consistent]
- **TikTok:** used as a search engine; it transcribes speech. [consistent]
- **Instagram:** max **5** hashtags per post. [consistent, check in the app]
- **X:** 1-2 hashtags at most. [consistent]

### 1.9 Keep people in the app
Links out of the app reduce reach. [consistent]
- **LinkedIn, Facebook:** put the link in the first comment, not in the post. [consistent]
- **X:** link clicks are a positive signal, but posts with links are reported to reach fewer people. [single]

### 1.10 Post regularly
Steady posting gives each app more signals to learn from. [consistent]
- **TikTok:** consistency matters more than the exact minute. [consistent]

### 1.11 No bait, no fake engagement
Engagement bait, pods and spammy hashtags are detected and penalised. [consistent]
- **LinkedIn:** pods and "comment YES" bait are penalised. [consistent]
- **TikTok:** "not interested" taps push similar content down. [official]

## 2. By group of apps

### 2.1 Short video: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels

**What they reward:** watch time, completion, rewatches, shares. A video goes to a small test
audience first and spreads only if they keep watching. [consistent]

**What works for the whole group:**
- A hook in the first 1-3 seconds: show the result or the question first.
- Short enough to finish and rewatch; a loop-friendly ending.
- On-screen text and spoken keywords (also helps viewers with the sound off).
- Original footage, no other app's watermark.
- Vertical 9:16.

| App | What works especially here | Hashtags | Best time (industry average) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Completion and rewatches [consistent]; keywords in caption, text and speech [consistent]; trending sound in its first ~24 h [single]; TikTok mixes creators and sounds, so vary your videos [official] | 3-5: trend + niche | 18:00-23:00 and weekends |
| **Instagram Reels** | Watch time and **sends** [official]; **Trial Reels** to test with non-followers first (1,000+ followers) [official]; original content only, since April 2026 [official] | Max **5** (trend, niche, branded) | Weekdays, late morning to early afternoon |
| **YouTube Shorts** | "Viewed vs. swiped away" in the first seconds gates reach [official metric]; Shorts are ranked apart from long videos [single]; originals over re-uploads [single] | 1-3; the title matters more | Evenings and weekends |
| **Facebook Reels** | Main way to reach non-followers; Meta added a "true interest" survey model in 2026 [single] | 0-3 | Tue-Wed 12:00-20:00 |

### 2.2 Photo and carousel feeds: Instagram feed, Facebook feed

**What they reward:** likes from people who already follow you, saves, sends, and time spent
swiping a carousel. [official for Instagram likes and sends]

**What works for the whole group:**
- Carousels: the first slide is the hook, the last slide asks to save or share.
- Useful or emotional content people want to keep (saves) or send (sends).
- Original photos. Reposts lose recommendations on Instagram [official].

| App | What works especially here | Hashtags | Best time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram feed** | Likes weigh more with followers, sends more with non-followers [official]; Feed, Explore and Stories rank separately, so a post can win on one and not another [official] | Max **5** | Weekdays, late morning to early afternoon |
| **Facebook feed** | Groups are treated as high-trust spaces [single]; repeat interactions from the same people help [single]; external links suppressed [consistent] | 0-3 | Tue-Wed 12:00-20:00; weekends weakest |

### 2.3 Conversation and text: X, Threads

**What they reward:** posts that start conversations and get reposted. [official for X]

**What works for the whole group:**
- A clear opinion, a question or something new: give people a reason to reply.
- Reply to the replies in the first hour.
- Short; one idea per post; threads for longer stories.

| App | What works especially here | Hashtags | Best time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X** | Public ranking code (`xai-org/x-algorithm`) predicts reposts, replies, follows [official]; one analysis: repost 20x, reply 13.5x, like 1x [single]; reply quality is scored [official] | 1-2 at most | Weekdays 08:00-11:00 |
| **Threads** | Replies and reposts carry posts to non-followers [consistent]; conversation starters do best | 0-1 topic tag | Same pattern as X (no solid study yet) |

### 2.4 Professional: LinkedIn

**What it rewards:** dwell time (how long people stop on the post) and real comments in the first
hour or two. [official for dwell time; consistent for comments]

**What works:**
- First two lines carry the point (before "see more").
- Personal experience or a concrete lesson beats generic advice.
- Answer every comment early; long comments help far more than reactions.
- Link in the first comment, not in the post [consistent].
- Avoid engagement pods, bait ("comment YES") and generic AI-sounding text [consistent].

| Hashtags | Best time |
|---|---|
| 3-5 specific professional topics | Weekdays 15:00-18:00; Wednesday and Thursday strongest |

## 3. Checklist before publishing

1. Does the first 1-3 seconds or first line state the point?
2. Is it short enough to finish? Would someone send it to a friend?
3. Is it original (no re-upload, no other app's watermark)?
4. Are the main keywords in the caption, on-screen text and speech?
5. Hashtags within the limit (Instagram max 5, X 1-2)?
6. No link in the post body on LinkedIn and Facebook?
7. Is the trend less than 48 hours old, or do we add a new angle?
8. Is it scheduled for when this account's followers are online, and is someone there to reply in
   the first hour?
