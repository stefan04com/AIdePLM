# Boabe: brand guidelines

Fictional demo brand for Moment. Used in every fit, draft and referee prompt.

## Who we are

Boabe is a specialty coffee brand from Bucharest: 6 coffee shops and a mobile app for ordering ahead. We roast in small batches and name the farm on every bag. People come to us to slow down for ten minutes, to work with a good cup next to them, or to grab a flat white on the way to the office.

## Voice

| We are | So we write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Warm | "Pull up a chair." | "Visit our location today." |
| Calm | Short sentences, no exclamation marks in a row | "HURRY!!! Limited time!!!" |
| Curious about coffee | "Washed Ethiopian, notes of apricot." | "The best coffee ever." |
| Quietly funny | A small wink, never a meme pile-on | Slang we wouldn't say out loud |
| Local | Bucharest streets, weather, rituals | Generic "big city" stock vibes |

Captions: max 2 short sentences plus an optional soft call to action. Max 3 hashtags. Emoji: at most one, never in place of words.

## Do

- Lead with the moment (the cold morning, the quiet corner, the first sip), not the product.
- Mention a product naturally, as part of the moment.
- Use soft calls to action: "See you at the bar.", "Order ahead in the app."
- Show real coffee-shop textures in visuals: ceramic cups, wood, steam, window light, autumn colours.

## Don't

- Hard sell: "Buy now", "Don't miss out", countdowns.
- Talk down to other coffee drinkers ("Still drinking instant?").
- Join trends about tragedies, politics, religion or real people's private lives.
- Use stock-looking visuals: fake smiles at the camera, generic skylines.

## Legal no-gos

These apply to every caption, hashtag and visual. Legal can block a post for any of them.

1. **No health or nutrition claims without documentation.** No "healthy", "healthiest", "boosts energy", "good for you", "detox". Nutrition claims such as "no added sugar", "sugar-free" or "low calorie" are regulated in the EU (Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006) and may only be used when Legal has the supporting documentation on file. As of today, **no menu item has approved documentation.**
2. **No superlatives or comparisons we can't prove:** "best", "#1", "the only", "better than", "the healthiest in town".
3. **No competitor names or recognisable competitor branding**, in text or visuals.
4. **No other brands' trademarks or characters**, including trademarked seasonal drink names. Our autumn drink is the **Pumpkin & Cinnamon Latte**; never call it anything else.
5. **No price or discount claims** that aren't in the current price list.
6. **No real people** (customers, staff or public figures) in generated visuals or captions without written consent.

## Audience

Mostly 20 to 40, living or working in central Bucharest: students and remote workers who stay a few hours, commuters who order ahead, and coffee enthusiasts who care about origin and brewing. The synthetic personas are built from `reviews.csv`.
